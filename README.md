#### Sage Media (`/portfolio/sage-media`)

**Tags:** Media · Web Development · UI/UX Design · Brand Identity
**Client:** Sage Media | **Industry:** Media & Publishing | **Duration:** 5 Weeks | **Year:** 2025

**Challenge:** An outdated web presence that didn't reflect their growth or credibility. Needed a complete rebuild with modern performance, SEO foundations and a CMS the non-technical team could manage independently.

**Workflow:**
1. Discovery & Content Audit (Week 1) — Mapped content, competitor benchmarking, defined site architecture.
2. UX Wireframing & Visual Design (Week 1–2) — Low/high-fidelity Figma designs matching Sage Media's brand identity.
3. Development (Week 2–4) — Built with React/Next.js, headless CMS integration, mobile-first, Core Web Vitals optimised.
4. SEO & Performance Optimisation (Week 4) — Technical SEO, meta structure, schema markup, sub-2-second load times.
5. QA & Launch (Week 5) — Cross-browser testing, DNS migration, zero-downtime go-live.

**Outcomes:**
- ~65% faster page load vs. old site
- 100% self-manageable CMS for editorial team
- All breakpoints mobile-responsive
- SEO foundation live from day one

**Testimonial:** *"SpaceGrey transformed our online presence. The website they built is sleek, fast and exactly what our brand needed."* — Rahul Arora, Founder, Sage Media
